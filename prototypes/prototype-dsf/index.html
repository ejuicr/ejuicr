    A quieter way to mix.

    The goal of ejuicr is to be a vaper's first choice for a convenient, easy-to-use e-juice calculator.

    Use it anywhere

    If it can load a web page, it can run ejuicr. The interface stays simple and unobtrusive, whether you're reaching past bottles for a keyboard or pecking at your phone with one hand.

    Little to no setup

    The faster you can start mixing, the better. Sign in to save your defaults so a new batch starts with your numbers already in place.

    No ads or trackers

    ejuicr is a labour of love. We don't want your money or your data. If there's anything we want from you, it's your feedback.

    No unnecessary emails

    We hate spam. You will never receive emails from us unless you ask for support or request a password reset.
