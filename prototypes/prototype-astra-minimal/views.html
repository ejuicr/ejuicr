About ejuicr

A convenient, easy-to-use ejuice calculator.

Use it anywhere

If it can load a web page, it can run ejuicr.

The interface stays simple and unobtrusive, whether you’re reaching past bottles for a keyboard or pecking at your phone with one hand.

Little to no setup time

The faster you can start mixing, the better. Sign in to save your defaults, including nicotine strength and PG/VG ratio, for next time.

No ads or trackers. Ever.

ejuicr is a labour of love. We don’t want your money or your data. If there’s anything we want from you, it’s your feedback.

No unnecessary emails

We hate spam. You won’t receive emails from us unless you email us for support or request a password reset.
